Olivia Rodrigo Live at the Empire Center

Written By    Gino Renzulli

On Friday, June 12, world-famous popstar, and regular Toluca Lake visitor,
Olivia Rodrigo held a surprise meet-and-greet for fans at the Target located in the Burbank Empire Center to celebrate the release of her new album You Seem Pretty Sad for a Girl So in Love. By all accounts, the event was only announced earlier that morning, inciting Rodrigo’s fans from all around the greater Los Angeles area to take an impromptu trip to Burbank that afternoon.

The scene at Target was much like you might have expected. There were about 200 excited fans standing in line outside on a warm
Burbank afternoon. The group was comprised largely of Rodrigo’s target demographic (young women), replete with fans dressed up in Rodrigo’s album cover outfit. Many sported a complimentary pink parasol provided by Target, too. That’s not to say that Rodrigo did not have male fans present. Sergio Gutierrez, for one, is a local content creator who secured one of the first places in line before going around and interviewing his fellow attendees. Gutierrez was just one element of an over jovial, sociable atmosphere.

Amidst the sea of younger fans, a few conspicuous fathers stood out as chaperones, including one among the second group in line. This father, who lives in Inglewood, told me that he works from home, and when his daughter saw a post about the meet-and-greet on Instagram, he packed up his laptop and got in the car at 11AM, arriving with his daughter around noon. Another father a little further down the line, a veteran of the USS Fitzgerald, shared with me that he had driven his daughter up from Irvine, at the drop of a hat as well, when she heard Olivia Rodrigo would be meeting fans about 50 miles from their home.

Inside the store, the meet-and-greet area was carved out of the Target tech section. A live DJ played Rodrigo’s latest album, as swarms of fans eagerly awaited her arrival, most of whom were not among the lucky few to arrive early enough for a chance to meet the pop sensation. They did, however, have an opportunity to purchase some limited-edition merchandise including alternative album covers and posters exclusively available at Target.

Excitement built as the crowd waited for Rodrigo to arrive, and when she emerged in front of the group, she was met with thunderous screams of joy. Quickly after arriving, she began her photo-ops just after 4PM. Olivia was wonderful with her fans. One-by-one, Olivia met her fans. No interaction felt rushed, and she was quick to share a smile, hug, and compliment with each and every fan. As fans gushed to Rodrigo about her music, style, and beauty, she handled each conversation graciously.

The process for photo handling was done as such. Fans were asked to have their camera app open and ready. If you were in line, when you approached the front of the line and Olivia was taking a photo with the fan ahead of you, you would hand your phone to its intermediary handler. This person would not be the one to take your photo, they would merely steward the phone for a while during your approach and pleasantries with Olivia. After this, the photographer would be handed your phone before taking your photo and returning it.

After all of this, fans would invariably come away giddy, smiling and squealing at the pictures with their favorite pop star. They say to never meet your heroes, but I’d venture to guess that Rodrigo’s devotees would disagree, as no one seemed to walk away disappointed.

It may seem easy in principle for Rodrigo to spend 90 minutes meeting people who love her and accepting compliments from them, but when you see it unfold in person, you recognize the professionalism that goes into making these interactions feel seamless. It is a craft in and of itself—and one Rodrigo has mastered.

There was a beautiful irony in this event taking place in the expanse that is The Empire Center Target. The building is so large that hundreds of shoppers probably went through the doors for the duration of Rodrigo’s time there and would not have even realized the event was going on, and not because Olivia didn’t draw a crowd; she certainly did.

But isn’t that the beauty of neighborhoods like Burbank and Toluca Lake? The world’s most famous stars could be on the other side of the store, and you’d never know it. Check out Olivia Rodrigo’s new album, You Seem Pretty Sad for a Girl So in Love, wherever you listen to music!
