The Tolucan Times: Ethics and Corrections

Written By    Editorial Board

July 2026

Our publication is committed to accurate, fair, and responsible journalism. We strive to report the truth, verify our information, and present stories with honesty and integrity. As a local publication, we are dedicated to serving and uplifting our community by highlighting the people, businesses, organizations, and events that make it unique.

Despite our best efforts, mistakes can happen. When we become aware of a factual error, we will correct it as promptly as possible. Corrections will be published in the following print issue and, when applicable, updated on our website or other online platforms.

If you believe we have published an error, please contact our editorial team with the relevant information so we can review the matter promptly.
